Your gift supports the charitable work of the International Charitable Foundation of Oleksandr Andriuk (МБФ Олександра Андріюка) in Rivne, Ukraine: aid for service members, children, internally displaced people, hospitals, and animals.

Donate online

The button below opens the secure WayForPay page “Допомога Українцям” (Help for Ukrainians). You choose the amount and the currency. Card details are entered on the payment service’s own page, not on this website.

Before you confirm, check the recipient, the amount, and the payment purpose. Please read the charitable donation terms and the privacy policy. Both documents are in Ukrainian.

Donate via WayForPay

After a successful payment you should reach the thank-you page. Keep your WayForPay receipt. Opening this button is not itself a donation. A Ukrainian confirmation page is also available: дякуємо за підтримку.

The WayForPay collection link does not accept a return address as a URL parameter. In the WayForPay cabinet (payment tools / store notifications), set the Approved URL or Return URL to https://andriukfoundation.com/thank-you.html. WayForPay uses one return address for this collection. Donors who prefer Ukrainian can open https://andriukfoundation.com/dyakuyemo-za-pidtrymku.html from the thank-you page.

What your donation supports

The foundation runs six ongoing charitable programs. Collected funds and in-kind help are used only for those purposes. Program pages are in Ukrainian.

  • Military aid — equipment, medicines, tactical medical supplies, warm clothing, and essentials for units, plus help for wounded service members and their families.
  • Dental care for service members — free dental treatment and prosthetics for Ukrainian service members, provided with the foundation’s partner dentists.
  • Children — support for orphans and children facing hardship because of the war, economic instability, or family difficulties.
  • Displaced people — help for internally displaced people with housing, work, medical access, and humanitarian kits.
  • Hospitals — equipment, supplies, and other resources for medical facilities working under extra strain.
  • Animals — rescue, treatment, and care for stray animals.

For a bank transfer, or a gift restricted to one program, confirm the details by email before you pay.

The foundation is a non-governmental charity. It does not run for profit and does not distribute income to its founder, governing bodies, or staff. Founder and director Oleksandr Andriuk works as a volunteer. The registered office is a legal address, not a place to collect or drop off aid without arranging it first.

How gifts are used

Published updates and the contact for questions are in the reports section. Video reports are on a separate page. To ask about your own gift, write the date, the amount, and the subject of your question. Do not send a full card number, CVV, or confirmation codes.

If a payment does not go through, check its status with your bank and with WayForPay before you pay again.

Other ways to help

You can offer goods, transport, volunteer time, or professional services. Agree the current need with the foundation before you collect or ship anything.
