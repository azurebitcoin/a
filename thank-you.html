Thank you for your donation

Українською

Your charitable gift helps the International Charitable Foundation of Oleksandr Andriuk (МБФ Олександра Андріюка) support service members, children, internally displaced people, hospitals, and animals. EDRPOU 41481057.

If you just finished paying

Keep your WayForPay receipt or confirmation. The foundation uses collected funds only for its statutory charitable purposes. Published updates are in the reports section.

If the payment did not finish, or you opened this page by accident, you can return to the donation page. Viewing this page is not itself a donation.
