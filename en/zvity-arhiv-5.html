Help for displaced people17 February 2025

Help for displaced people

A displaced woman from the occupied town of Ocheretyne in Donetsk Oblast, Olesia Tolstaia, visited our humanitarian headquarters.
In the spring of 2022, Olesia turned to our Foundation for help through a UN agency hotline.
That is how our acquaintance began) Later, Olesia, together with her husband, was already helping at the headquarters to provide aid to displaced people just like themselves)☝️
At the moment, the family’s life has stabilized in Kyiv; they have had a wonderful baby girl and now turn to us as friends❤️
We provided aid in the form of a stroller for the little one, diapers, baby food, brand-new little outfits for the daughter, various treats for the older child, vitamins for the whole family, and sneakers for mom)
