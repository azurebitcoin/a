Military aid14 February 2025

Aid to various locations

1,500 kg of aid (food, hygiene products, energy drinks, medicines and supplies, clothing and footwear, books, toys, baby food, diapers, and of course treats 🙏) was sent in just the past day by the Andriuk Foundation (МБФ Олександра Андріюка) “Victory and the Future” ☝️, and that is 3 flat boxes, 4 pallets, a wooden structure with baby food, and separate parcels.
Aid received by:
– a military hospital in the Cherkasy region🏥 that operates on the wounded around the clock ;
– an animal shelter in the Kharkiv region 🐕🐈‍⬛
– displaced people in the city of Bohodukhiv, Kharkiv Oblast 👨‍👩‍👧‍👧👫;
– Service members (Kramatorsk, Donetsk axis) 🔰🥷🏿💪;
– and individual requests from fellow volunteers 😉.
We sincerely thank all our partners and donors, who have already become friends to us🙏. You are the best proof of the unity of the Ukrainian people🇺🇦🇺🇦🇺🇦

Help for hospitals14 February 2025

Municipal enterprise “Rivne Regional Phthisiopulmonology Medical Center”

The team of our Foundation’s volunteers in the city of Rivne visited the Municipal Enterprise “Rivne Oblast Phthisiopulmonology Medical Center” to provide humanitarian aid. The center also receives internally displaced persons from Ukraine’s hot spots.
At its request, the hospital received from our foundation medicines, consumables, food, hygiene products, and much more, and for the little ones they handed over “The Christian Alphabet” 😇
Every request matters to our team, because our mission is to be where we are needed most !❤️🙏

Help for children14 February 2025

A celebration at the Evangelical Church “Spiritual Revival”

Friends, today we are sharing a joyful event with you — holding a celebration for more than 200 children from Ukraine’s hot spots (Donetsk Oblast, Zaporizhzhia, and Kherson Oblast) 😇

The event took place at the Evangelical Church “Spiritual Revival”, with the participation of the head of the community, Roman Ovcharenko.
For the Foundation’s team it is an honor to receive such invitations, to have the chance to be part of the children’s happiness, to see their sincere smiles, and to feel incredible happiness together with them 🥹🥰
Each of our volunteers did their utmost so that the children would forget, at least for a couple of hours, the events each of them had to live through because of the terrible events of the war…😥

Help for children14 February 2025

Rivne children’s home

Today the team of the Rivne branch of the Andriuk Foundation (МБФ Олександра Андріюка) “Victory and the Future” visited the Rivne Children’s Home with humanitarian aid. Sincere thanks to everyone who helps us support the little Ukrainians 💙💛
