We help service members, children, and displaced people

The International Charitable Foundation of Oleksandr Andriuk accepts requests for aid and charitable contributions. We work in a targeted way: equipment and medicines for service members, support for children, humanitarian aid for IDPs, hospitals, and animals. EDRPOU 41481057.

Common questions

How to get help?

Write to info@andriukfoundation.com or call +38 (098) 656-79-35: name, city, a contact for a reply, and a short description of the need. The foundation will clarify the request and the procedure for submitting an application. Whether aid is possible depends on resources; it must be agreed before a visit. On the previous website the foundation also mentioned requests via Facebook Messenger at official page — this is an additional channel, not a replacement for the official email and phone.

How to hand over items or a humanitarian shipment?

First contact the foundation, state the list, condition, quantity of items, and city of dispatch. Agree on the need, the delivery, and the recipient before sending.

How to become a volunteer or a partner?

Send to the official email what you can help with, in which city, and when you are available. For an organization, add the name and the contact of the responsible person.

Where to check the work and the donations?

Open foundation reports or video reports. For a request about a specific program or period, write to the official email. The terms of contributions are set out in the public offer agreement.

About the organization

We are a nonprofit charitable organization

Charitable organization “International Charitable Foundation of Oleksandr Andriuk” (Благодійна організація «Міжнародний благодійний фонд Олександра Андріюка») — a nonprofit, non-governmental charitable organization. The Foundation has been helping people in need and abandoned children since 2016 and was officially registered in Ukraine in 2017 (legal entity identification code 41481057).

All funds collected and material aid received are directed exclusively to statutory charitable purposes. The Foundation’s founder and head, Oleksandr Andriuk, works on a volunteer basis.
