Foundation programs

DonateGet help

Six ongoing charitable programs. We provide aid upon request: a person, an organization, a military unit, or a hospital sends an application. The procedure for first contact is on the “Help” page.

Military aid

Every day Ukrainian soldiers risk their lives, defending our freedom and future. The foundation raises funds and purchases equipment, medicines, and essentials, and also delivers humanitarian cargo at the request of units.

Program page

Dental care for service members

Service members often have no access to quality medical services — both on the front line and after they return. The foundation has brought together caring dentists, volunteers, and donors to provide free dental care.

Program page

Help for children

Support for children who, because of the war, economic instability, or family difficulties, have found themselves in difficult life circumstances, including orphans. Besides material aid, the foundation holds festive events for children deprived of parental care.

Program page

Help for hospitals

Hospitals and medical centers are working at an intensified pace, often without adequate resources. The foundation delivers equipment, consumables, and other resources needed to help patients.

Program page

Help for displaced people

Support for internally displaced persons: finding housing, employment, access to medical services, and humanitarian kits for families who have left their homes.

Program page

Help for animals

Rescue, treatment, and care for homeless animals that cannot protect themselves.
