Help for children

DonateGet help

More photos of this program in the gallery

Every day our team makes an effort to support the most vulnerable — children who, because of armed conflicts, economic instability, or family difficulties, have found themselves in difficult life circumstances.

We founded this charitable foundation in order to unite caring people around a single goal — to create, for every child, conditions in which they can grow, learn, and develop in safety.

Main areas of the children’s program:

  • Support for educational and rehabilitation centers, boarding institutions, and children’s homes.
  • Help for children with special educational needs (disability, cerebral palsy, autism).
  • Organizing festive charity events and giving gifts to orphans.

How to apply regarding this program

For parents, legal representatives of children, and representatives of children’s institutions.

What to include in the first request

The name of the adult applicant, the city, a contact for a reply, the child’s age, and a description of the need. For an institution — its name and the contact of the responsible person.

How to offer help

To offer clothing, hygiene products, toys, or learning materials, state the quantity, the condition of the items, and the age group. Before sending, agree the need and the address.

Send a request to the official email or call. Whether aid is possible, and its scope and time frame, depend on available resources and on confirmation of the request.
