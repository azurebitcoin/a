Help for children16 February 2025

Infant home in the city of Rivne

Today the team of the International Charitable Foundation of Oleksandr Andriuk sent aid to the infant children’s home in the city of Rivne, which we have been looking after for more than 8 years 💞
For the very tiniest children (from 0 to 3 years), the following were purchased and sent on request: Humana baby food, wet wipes, disposable underpads, diapers, sweets, toys and building sets, baby overalls, and polyester-fill blankets.
For the bedridden children who need palliative care and are in this children’s home, bedding, blankets, adult diapers, and sweets were sent 🛍️🧸

Help for children16 February 2025

A celebration at the children’s home in the city of Oleksandriia

Little ones… all alone…😭
They have no one,
These little eyes are always waiting for us volunteers …
They wait, and they hope,
That we will give a happy moment …

Friends, before you is a report from the volunteers of our Foundation who visited the little ones, already so dear, at the children’s home in the city of Oleksandriia, Rivne Oblast 👫👫👫.
Thanks to our volunteers, patrons, friends, and caring people who helped raise money to help children, we were able to cover stationery needs for 41 children for the new school year.
Of course, there were also individual gifts for every child from 3 to 16 years old, painting pictures and puzzles for the very smallest 👶🏻, sweets 🍬🍭🍮🍨, juices 🧃🥤, and fruit 🍇🍊🍏 👏
