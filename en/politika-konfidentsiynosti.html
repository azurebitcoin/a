Privacy policy

Rules for collecting, processing, and protecting personal data

1. General provisions

This Privacy Policy is an integral part of the terms of use of the Site concluded between the visitor and the Charitable Foundation regarding use of the website https://andriukfoundation.com.

This Policy describes the types of personal information we receive from visitors to our Site, and the procedure for collecting, processing, and using it in accordance with the requirements of the current legislation of Ukraine.

Your providing or posting of information on the Site is your agreement with the content of this Privacy Policy and your permission for your data to be collected and used.

2. Types of information we receive

Personal data: We may collect information that makes it possible to identify you (name, telephone number, email address, and so on) when you voluntarily provide it through feedback or contact forms.

Technical data: To measure Site traffic, the Site uses Google Analytics 4. Analytics cookies are activated only after you give consent through the cookie settings banner. Google may process technical data about the browser, device, pages viewed, and interaction with the Site in accordance with its privacy policy. You may reject analytics cookies or change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” button.

Payment information: We do not collect bank card data directly. All payments and charitable contributions are processed through secure certified payment systems (for example, WayForPay).

3. Use of information

The information received is used exclusively to organize feedback with you, to send informational messages, to carry out charitable activities, and to keep records of requests. We do not sell Personal Data and do not transfer it to third parties without lawful grounds.

4. Data security

The Foundation takes all necessary technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or you want to withdraw consent to the processing of data, please contact us at:
Email: info@andriukfoundation.com
