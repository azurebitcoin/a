Dentistry4 February 2025

The first free dental clinic in Rivne

The International Charitable Foundation of Oleksandr Andriuk is opening the FIRST FREE dental clinic in the city of Rivne for service members 
The Foundation is able to provide assistance in dental prosthetics for service members who need treatment! At present it is possible to receive up to five patients a day, but the number of appointments will be increased.
Contact details for inquiries will be added later.
Thank God that we have the chance to carry out one more important project
