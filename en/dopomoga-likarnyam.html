Help for hospitals

DonateGet help

More photos of this program in the gallery

Every day hospitals and medical centers find themselves on the front line in the fight for human life and health. In times of acute trial they become an outpost of hope, where medics work at an intensified pace, often without adequate resources and equipment.

We launched a charitable initiative in order to unite the efforts of caring people who want to provide hospitals with everything necessary to give patients quality care.

Join our initiative: by donating funds, spreading information, or becoming a volunteer. Together we can give medics a better chance of saving human lives, and give our hospitals modern conditions for providing emergency care. Your concern is a substantial contribution to strengthening the health of our nation.

DonateGet help

How to apply regarding this program

For heads and authorized representatives of medical institutions.

What to include in the first request

The name of the institution, the city, the contact of the responsible person, a list of materials or equipment, the quantity, and the required specifications. An official request will help clarify the need.

How to offer help

Partners should state the completeness of the set, the technical condition, the shelf life of consumables, and whether delivery is possible. Acceptance and compatibility are agreed with the institution.

Send a request to the official email or call. Whether aid is possible, and its scope and time frame, depend on available resources and on confirmation of the request.
