Military aid

DonateGet help

More photos of this program in the gallery

Every day Ukrainian soldiers risk their lives, defending our freedom and future. To provide them with everything they need, our foundation is announcing a collection of funds and humanitarian cargo.

We purchase and deliver directly to the front line specialized equipment, medicines, tactical medicine supplies, warm clothing, and essentials. Every contribution is a step toward our shared victory and toward preserving the lives of our defenders.

What exactly we purchase and deliver:

  • Medical kits, medicines, and vitamins for military units on the front line.
  • Tactical gear and protective equipment.
  • Aid for wounded fighters and their families in hospitals.
  • Specialized requests from military units in line with official applications.

How to ask for help or to help service members?

Military units and hospitals can apply to the foundation with an official request. To support the collection, you can make a charitable contribution or contact our volunteers.

How to apply regarding this program

For military units, hospitals, and representatives of units.

What to include in the first request

The name of the organization or unit, the contact of the responsible person, and a list and quantity of the items needed. The place and method of handover are agreed separately; do not include the coordinates of positions in an open request.

How to offer help

If you are offering medical kits, warm clothing, or other material aid, state the quantity, condition, and city of dispatch.

Send a request to the official email or call. Whether aid is possible, and its scope and time frame, depend on available resources and on confirmation of the request.
