Foundation program

Dental care for service members

DonateGet help

Program video

More photos of this program in the gallery

Our defenders’ health is a comprehensive matter. Our foundation is carrying out a program of free dental care for service members, provided by the Foundation’s partner doctors.

Service members on the front line are often unable to receive quality, qualified dental care. Thanks to our foundation’s initiative and the support of dental doctors, we organize free treatment and prosthetics for Ukrainian soldiers.

How to apply regarding this program

For service members who are requesting dental care.

What to include in the first request

Name, a contact for a reply, the city where you are staying, and a short description of the need. The doctor’s availability, the scope of treatment, and the appointment need to be agreed with the foundation.

How to offer help

Doctors and clinics can write which services they are ready to provide, and in which city. Send medical documents only after agreeing this with the responsible specialist.

Send a request to the official email or call. Whether aid is possible, and its scope and time frame, depend on available resources and on confirmation of the request.
