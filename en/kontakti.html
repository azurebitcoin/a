Request help

  1. Please read request procedure and choose an area of aid and briefly describe your need.
  2. Write to info@andriukfoundation.com or call +38 (098) 656-79-35. In the email, state your name, city, and a contact for the reply.
  3. Agree with the foundation the application, the documents required, and a possible way of receiving the aid. Do not send things, and do not plan a visit, until it is agreed.

The legal address is not a confirmed aid pickup point. Acceptance, availability of resources, and time frames need to be confirmed by phone.

Support the foundation

You can make a charitable donation, offer material aid, or volunteer. For things or a humanitarian shipment, indicate the list, the quantity, the condition, and the city of dispatch.

An online donation is made on the WayForPay page. Before paying, check the recipient and read the the public offer agreement. Bank details for a separate transfer can be confirmed through the foundation’s official email.

Prepare a letter to the foundation

Fill in the fields to prepare the text of the request. After confirmation you will go to a thank-you page, from which you can send the letter from your own email. The text remains in your browser. Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

Do not add copies of documents, full medical data, or military coordinates. Please read the the privacy policy.
