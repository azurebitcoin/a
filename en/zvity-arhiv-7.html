Military aid13 February 2025

Aid at six military positions

Sunday turned out fruitful 😌
The Andriuk Foundation (МБФ Олександра Андріюка) “Victory and the Future” is shipping 915 kg of humanitarian aid — imagine, almost a tonne ☝️ — namely: energy drinks, food, baby food, snacks, and much more, for civilians and service members along 6 routes 💪😎:
– Sloviansk (Bakhmut axis), Donetsk Oblast;
– Zhovti Vody;
– Kharkiv;
– Mykolaiv;
– Pavlohrad;
– Selydove (Donetsk Oblast)

Aid for orphaned children and children evacuated from hot spots in Ukraine

Friends, on the eve of the most important Christian holiday — Easter🙏
Novopecherska School in the city of Kyiv collected treats for orphaned children and children evacuated from hot spots in Ukraine.

Help for children11 February 2025

The foundation visited the Dzherelo Children’s Center

Friends! Ukrainians!
This is an incredible day 💥🙌. Thanks to the organizer @katya.krasnikova and our volunteer @soloha_official, our Foundation visited the Children’s Center “Dzherelo” @dzherelo_medical and greeted children from the hot spots of Donetsk Oblast (Bakhmut, Soledar, Torets, and others) who have been left orphans.
