Help for displaced people

DonateGet help

More photos of this program in the gallery

Every day thousands of families are forced to leave their own homes, fleeing danger. Those who find themselves in an unfamiliar city without basic resources need our support.

We provide support for internally displaced people: from finding housing to humanitarian kits and employment.

How to apply regarding this program

For people and families who were forced to leave their place of residence.

What to include in the first request

The city where you are now, the number of family members, the most urgent need, and a contact for a reply. Briefly state whether the request concerns humanitarian items, housing, or a consultation.

How to offer help

If you can provide things or offer partnership help, write what exactly, in what quantity, and in which city it is available. Whether support is available needs to be confirmed before the trip.

Send a request to the official email or call. Whether aid is possible, and its scope and time frame, depend on available resources and on confirmation of the request.
